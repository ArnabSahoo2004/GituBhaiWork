About Our Kabuli Chickpeas

Chickpeas, also known as garbanzo beans or Kabuli Chana, are one of the world's oldest cultivated crops and a nutritional powerhouse. India is the largest producer of chickpeas globally, and the Kabuli variety — with its large, round, cream-coloured seeds — is the most sought-after in international markets.

Our chickpeas are grown under certified organic conditions with no synthetic pesticides or fertilisers. The crop is rain-fed or irrigated with clean water sources and processed at our state-of-the-art facility which includes optical colour sorting, gravity separation, and pneumatic cleaning to achieve best-in-class quality.

Ideal For: Hummus production, canned chickpea manufacturing, curries (Chana Masala), salads, roasted snacks, flour (besan/gram flour), and Middle Eastern & Mediterranean cuisines.
