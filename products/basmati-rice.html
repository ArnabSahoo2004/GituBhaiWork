About Our Basmati Rice

Basmati Rice is the crown jewel of Indian agriculture — a uniquely aromatic long-grain rice variety grown exclusively in the Himalayan foothills. The word "Basmati" translates to "fragrant" in Sanskrit, and the aroma of properly aged Basmati is unmistakable.

At Sur Basant Overseas, we source only from GI-tagged Basmati zones and work with farmers who practice traditional, sustainable cultivation methods. Our rice is naturally sun-dried, mechanically cleaned, and stored in temperature-controlled silos to preserve freshness and aroma.

The aging process — a minimum of 12 months — is what separates premium Basmati from ordinary rice. Aging reduces moisture content, allowing each grain to elongate to 2–3× its original length upon cooking, producing perfectly separate, fluffy grains with that signature nutty fragrance.

Ideal For: Biryani, Pulao, Fried Rice, Khichdi, international gourmet dishes, hotel & restaurant supply, retail packaging.
