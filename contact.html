Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions from our export customers.

Our standard MOQ is 1 Metric Ton (MT) for most products. However, we can accommodate smaller sample orders (50–100 kg) for new customers evaluating our quality. For commodities like premium spices and dried fruits, the MOQ may vary. Please contact us for specific product MOQs.

Every shipment is accompanied by a comprehensive documentation package including: Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, Lab Analysis Report (pesticide residue, aflatoxin, heavy metals), Organic Certificate (if applicable), FSSAI License Copy, and Packing List. We can also provide USDA and EU organic certificates for certified shipments.
We typically accept: 30% advance payment + 70% against shipping documents (T/T), Letter of Credit (LC) at sight, and for established clients, open account terms can be discussed. We accept payments in USD, EUR, GBP, and INR via SWIFT bank transfer.
Absolutely. We offer full private labelling and custom packaging services. We can pack in your branded pouches, bags, or boxes in sizes from 250g to 50kg. Minimum order for custom packaging is typically 5 MT. Our design team can help with label design compliant with your country's food labelling regulations.
We ship from all major Indian ports including JNPT (Nhava Sheva), Mundra, Kandla, Chennai, Kolkata, and Vizag. We also handle inland container depot (ICD) stuffing at Delhi, Ludhiana, and Jaipur for Northern India origin goods. We can arrange FCL and LCL shipments as per your requirement.
