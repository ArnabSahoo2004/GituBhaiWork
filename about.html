Our Story

From a Small Farm to a Global Brand

Sur Basant Overseas Private Limited was founded over two and a half decades ago with a simple belief — that the world deserves the best quality food, grown naturally and delivered with integrity.

What started as a small family trading business in the heartland of India has grown into a trusted name in international agri-export. Today, we serve customers in over 30 countries, supplying premium-grade grains, pulses, spices, oilseeds, dried fruits and processed foods.

Our roots remain firmly in the soil — we work directly with thousands of farmers across India, ensuring fair trade practices, sustainable farming, and the highest quality standards at every step.

Founded in 1999 with a mission to bring the finest Indian produce to the world
Directly partnered with 2,000+ farmers across Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan & UP
Exporting to 30+ countries across Asia, Europe, Africa, and the Americas
ISO 22000, FSSAI, APEDA and USDA Organic certified operations
